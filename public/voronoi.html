DiscretePoint

A Head-Pointing Research Study

What you'll do

Complete a short calibration, then navigate through two rounds of a head-pointing task. No mouse or keyboard is required.

How it works

Your webcam tracks your head orientation. Point your head toward a region on screen to activate it.

Duration

Approximately 5–10 minutes.

** Make sure your face is fully visible in the webcam before continuing.

or press Space

Stage 1: Calibration

1

A dot will appear at 20 different positions on the screen. Move your head to look directly at each dot.

2

When your head is steady and centered on the dot, press Space to capture that position.

3

Once the 20 dots are completed, highlighted regions will appear on the screen. Look directly at each highlighted region within the given time.

or press Space

Stage 2: Head-Pointing Task

The green region is your current target. Point your head toward it to activate it.

The yellow region shows where you're going next.

Hold your head still in the green region for a moment to confirm your selection.

If you activate the wrong region, it will flash red. Keep going and try to reach the correct one.

Move as quickly and accurately as you can. There are two rounds with a short break in between, followed by a repeat of both rounds with no grace period for look-aways.
